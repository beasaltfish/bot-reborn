# BME280 breakout selected for the simulator

The physical reference is the Adafruit BME280 STEMMA QT breakout, product 2652,
not an unspecified four-pin BME280 module. The manufacturer lists dimensions of
25.2 × 18.0 × 4.6 mm. The seven 2.54 mm header positions, left to right when
viewed as in the manufacturer guide, are VIN, 3Vo, GND, SCK, SDO, SDI, CS.
The extra STEMMA QT connectors are not yet modeled as connection points.

For I²C, SCK is SCL and SDI is SDA. VIN accepts 3–5 V and should match the
controller's logic voltage; the breakout has a 3.3 V regulator and level
shifting. 3Vo is a regulated output, not a second supply input. The default
address is 0x77; closing the ADDR jumper or connecting SDO to GND selects
0x76. This simulator's address setting represents the solder jumper. Physical
SDO wiring and conflicts with that setting still require electrical modeling.

The Bosch chip's datasheet current figures are chip-only and operating-mode
dependent; they are not a measured module-wide idle/peak budget. Do not treat
them as a complete breakout current model. Module power, I²C and readings are
not yet enabled in the workbench; the entry is marked as in development.
