# Making a Short

What the first [YouTube Short](https://youtube.com/shorts/SNA9ok1j0Sg) (2026-10-06) taught us, so the next one does not
start from zero. Edited in CapCut; one 19-second phone clip of the robot on
the floor, a child leaning in, and the real dog eating in its pen.

---

## The edit

**Check for baked-in letterbox first.** Crop to 16:9 on the picture, *then* set Canvas /
Background → **Blur**. Next time, shoot vertical, or check before editing.

**Do not stretch to fill.** Keep the whole picture over a blurred background. Keyframes can
pan from the robot to the child if you zoom.

**Blur, not Pattern or Color.** 

**The first two seconds decide everything.** Start on the bark or on the
child's reaction, not on the robot sitting still. Cut the shaky end where the
camera drops to the floor.

**Keep the original audio.** The bark is the punchline. No voice-over; music,
if any, sits well under the bark.

**Effects: almost none.** At most a punch-in zoom on the bark. No
transitions, stickers or filters, because a homemade clip that looks like an
ad loses what made it worth watching.

**Shoot a close-up next time.** The robot is small in a wide shot, and a first
viewer sees a box on the floor. Two or three seconds of the phone's face and
the tracks moving tells them what it is.

## The joke

The best thing in the clip was not planned: the real dog ignored everyone
and kept eating while the robot played with the child. That contrast is the
title. A joke that depends on noticing the dog needs the dog **large and
clear in the crop**, because a Shorts title is a small line that most viewers
never read. An on-screen caption carries it better than the title alone does.

## Title and description

Title used: **Real dog: busy eating 🐶 Robot: on duty 🤖**

Description used:

```
I turned a $4 toy car into an AI robot for my kid.
An old phone rides on top as its brain — it listens, talks, drives, and barks.
The real dog had other priorities.

How it works: phone → WebUSB → FT232H → motors. Voice in, AI decides, car moves.
Open source: https://github.com/beasaltfish/bot-reborn

#robot #DIY #maker #AI #robotdog
```

The first line is the hook, the third line carries the joke, and the repo link
is what makes the video send people to the project.
