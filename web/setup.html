Connectivity test

This is the instrument, not the way in. Setting up three providers for the first time happens in the robot now — a sheet with three keys in it and a test beside each one. What is here that is not there: fixtures in your own voice, so a pass means the chain understands you and not just that the key is valid; the typed driver, which reaches brain.js without a microphone; and every field editable at once, which is what comparing one provider against another needs (spec §12 ⑪). The two write the same config, so either can be used to fix what the other set.

Fixtures

The STT test reads three fixed clips, and they have to be your own voice: somebody else's accent cannot find your problem. Say each line as written, then listen back before trusting it — a clip where the sentence got cut off still transcribes into something plausible. Recordings are kept in this browser, never in the repo, so clearing site storage means recording them again. The microphone stays open from the first Record until you leave the page. A .wav put into web/fixtures/ with ffmpeg is used when there is no recording — see web/fixtures/README.md.

    Connectivity tests

    Every one of these tests the hardest case on purpose — see spec §9.3.

    1. Reads the three clips from Fixtures above: Chinese, English, and the two mixed inside one sentence. All three must come back with a transcript. A clip that was never recorded fails this test rather than being skipped quietly.

    2. It has to answer and to call a tool. Those are two different things, verified separately.

    3. Synthesises 「『往前走』的英文是 go forward」. Both languages have to be intelligible.

    4. 200 ms forward; the car twitches.

    Typed driver

    Skips the microphone and feeds text straight to brain.js — the whole v1 chain except for STT. Connect the FT232H above first.
