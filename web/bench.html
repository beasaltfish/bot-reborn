Calibration bench

Status: not connected

① Polarity: is side A left or right?

Each button holds its pins high for 600 ms. Watch which way the wheels turn, and write the answer into docs/hardware.md.

② Byte rate and road speed

At 1200 baud, write 3000 forward bytes plus one stop byte, all in one go. Time the motor with a stopwatch; measure how far the car travelled. If the car is about to leave the bench, hit “Emergency stop” above — for those 20 seconds JS is doing nothing at all, and that button is the only thing that can interrupt it.

⑧ The motor's starting threshold

Start at 100 ms and add 50 ms each round, ten tries each. The answer is the smallest pulse that moves the car every single time.
